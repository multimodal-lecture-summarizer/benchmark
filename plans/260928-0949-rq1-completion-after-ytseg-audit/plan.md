## Overview

This plan continues the RQ1 work after two verified results: the [35-video MIT001 paired diagnostic](../../multimodal-lecture-summarizer/plans/reports/rq1-mit001-multimodal-260928-0909-paired-diagnostic-report.md) has already run visual, acoustic and full heuristic fusion; the [YTSeg rights/split audit](../../multimodal-lecture-summarizer/plans/reports/research-260928-0936-ytseg-video-rights-channel-split-report.md) found uncleared source-video rights and severe channel overlap in official splits. Do not rerun completed variants merely to change `not_run` labels in older prose. Learned C5/C6 remain `not_run` without eligible training data and checkpoint provenance.

There are **two deliverables with different claim levels**:

1. Close the MIT001 local **single-course diagnostic** on the existing 35 videos: preserve aggregate results, provenance, nine `review` media rows and prior exposure of every label. It cannot become a held-out or cross-course result by reshuffling these 35 IDs. The [MIT001 rights audit](../../multimodal-lecture-summarizer/plans/reports/260928-0839-mit001-video-rights-media-audit-report.md) already permits the scoped aggregate write-up while leaving asset redistribution and per-file rights unresolved.
2. Pursue an **official multimodal RQ1** only if an independently sourced cohort passes per-video rights/access, chapter GT, course/source-disjoint split and D2 gates. YTSeg is a candidate, not a presumed eligible video source. If it fails, record a `blocked_rights`, `blocked_data` or `blocked_gt` decision and stop official execution. The completed MIT001 diagnostic remains a valid scoped output.

## Decision gates

| Gate | Pass evidence | Failure outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic closeout | Existing 35/35 receipts and paired tables reconcile; claim and release scope documented | Correct report/receipt mismatch; no new score claim |
| Video rights/access | Per-selected-video rights, platform access path and exception status recorded | `blocked_rights` or `blocked_access`; no bulk acquisition |
| Independent cohort | Verified `course_id`/source groups, no duplicate IDs or groups across train/validation/test, adequate group count and GT | `blocked_data` or `blocked_gt`; no held-out/course-generalization claim |
| Pre-registered design | Master-plan amendment records any cohort, split, GT, metric or primary-source change, including a custom split of the YTSeg CC BY subset | `blocked_protocol`; no D2 or final-test run |
| D2/protocol | Immutable IDs/hashes, GT origin, rights ledger, eligibility matrix, config and final-test seal | `blocked_d2`; no official 04a/04b |
| Official run | Same-unit multimodal features and predictions, validation-only selection, once-only final test, complete failure denominator | Report actual `failed`/`not_run`, never synthesize metrics |

The 538 MITFLD rows and 468 lecture-like metadata candidates are discovery pools, not full-audit targets. Preserve attrition and any predeclared reserve rule; do not backfill based on model results.

Amend the [master plan](../../multimodal-lecture-summarizer/plans/260901-unified-scientific-benchmark/framework/00-master-plan.md) before **any** change to its pre-registered YTSeg official split plus frozen multimodal-subset design, including a custom split of the CC BY subset, cohort filtering, GT origin, metric, primary dataset or scientific claim.
