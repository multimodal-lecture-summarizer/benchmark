# RQ1 bounded recruitment: ten independent groups

**Locked 2026-09-30, before inspecting media for this batch.** This is a fixed recruitment batch for the seven-group shortfall against the prospective 20-group floor. It is not a cohort or split. No model prediction or metric may determine which row survives.

Selection used the existing Open Yale course inventory, source lecture pages, and prior source-only qualification records. Include one unreviewed lecture in each of ten distinct course and lecturer components, excluding every course or lecturer among the 13 qualified groups. Prefer lecture/board presentation and courses without a known third-party-heavy selected lecture. Where an earlier lecture in that same course was held for boundary alignment, choose the listed alternative lecture without moving or editing its GT. The ten exact URLs and order are frozen in `rq1-recruitment-260930-ten-group-locked.csv`; no replacement after media review. Stop qualification when seven *additional* independent groups pass; record the unreviewed rows as not run. If the fixed batch is exhausted below 20 total passes, set `blocked_data` and amend the scope and precision target before any final test.

The source criterion is publisher-native internal chapter starts on the selected lecture page. The media criterion is a publisher-linked, accessible full lecture with exact identity, byte/hash receipt and full A/V decode. The rights criterion is component-specific scoped noncommercial research use under [Open Yale terms](https://oyc.yale.edu/terms); its general CC license does not cover uncredited third-party components. The GT criterion applies the existing unchanged ±3 s boundary rule to each original internal start and records title errors separately. A page, license or accessible file alone does not pass any unit. An unresolved component, media mismatch or boundary remains a hold; no GT shifts, title edits or candidate scores enter recruitment.

Course/lecturer independence is relative to the 13 existing pass groups. ECON 252 2008 is one Robert Shiller component; PHYS 200 is one Ramamurti Shankar component. Other offerings taught by either lecturer cannot form additional groups in this design.
