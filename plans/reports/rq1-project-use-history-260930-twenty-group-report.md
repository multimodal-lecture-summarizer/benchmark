This is supporting provenance, not a cryptographic checkpoint-to-input binding. The older aggregate RQ1 benchmark summary has no per-video IDs and cannot be treated as held-out evidence for this cohort. Before official use, the chosen checkpoint and candidate execution must provide a fresh, exact input/code provenance receipt and must not train or tune on final-test groups.

All 20 therefore pass this **bounded project-controlled history screen** for split assignment. The original YTSeg/VidChapters release split names, including `train`, are upstream metadata; they do not by themselves mean this project's model used a video. If a later run manifest reveals project development use of a proposed final-test group, the sealed split is invalid and the final test remains closed until a pretest correction. No candidate prediction or metric was inspected for this decision.
