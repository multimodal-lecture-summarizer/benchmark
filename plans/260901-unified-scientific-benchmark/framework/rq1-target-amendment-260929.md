# RQ1 target amendment: mixed YTSeg and VidChapters candidates

**Recorded 2026-09-29, before any mixed-cohort D2 freeze or official RQ1 run. Status: conditional amendment; source selection and split remain open.** This amends the master plan's YTSeg-only target assumption to allow a qualified VidChapters lecture subset under one boundary endpoint. It does not certify the three selected VidChapters videos as eligible.

## Endpoint and source representation

- RQ1 primary endpoint is detection of **internal chapter starts** on the complete video timeline. Primary metric is one-to-one collar F1 at ±3 s, macro by video with paired candidate comparison. Secondary ±5/10 s, Pk, and WindowDiff remain as in the [YTSeg protocol draft](../../../../plans/260929-1659-rq1-ytseg-evaluation-protocol/rq1-ytseg-evaluation-protocol-draft.md). Titles are provenance and QA only; title generation is outside this RQ1 endpoint.
- For YTSeg, parse the published, unmodified `target_ts` markers, validate starts and titles against its `raw_chapters`, and retain only starts strictly inside `(0,D)`. Do not build the target by zipping separate title and timestamp lists.
- For VidChapters, use the original paired `label,time` entries from hash-pinned `chapters.pkl`. `time` is the uploader supplied whole-video chapter start in seconds; `label` is the free-form title from the timestamp lines in the video description. Validate pair order, title, duration, description parity, and exact-media alignment per ID. Do not substitute `chapters_dvc_*`, derive an observed end label, or silently filter structural titles. Start 0 and end D are structural sentinels and do not earn a matched boundary.
- Keep `dataset_id`, source revision/hash, original record/row identity, annotation and probed media duration, media hash, timestamp convention, semantic QA decision, and GT label hashes in the unit provenance. No target field or derived gold boundaries may enter predictor inputs.
- Apply the [pre-score title-versus-boundary decision rule](rq1-boundary-qualification-rule-260929.md). A title error alone does not disqualify a correctly aligned boundary. Never move a published start, rewrite a label, or inspect model output to decide whether a unit survives qualification.

The [selected-group semantic check](../../../../plans/reports/vidchapters-semantic-260929-1818-selected-groups-report.md) confirmed 22/22 raw pairs across three selected IDs against stored description lines. The [VidChapters paper](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/9b5c3e00d6ed30aad7adac9e7a664de1-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf) establishes this intended meaning, not per-video alignment. Subsequent [exact-media qualification](../../../../plans/reports/vidchapters-media-260929-1840-two-priority-receipt.json) passed `AW3a2ECNFlE` on GT/media/scoped research rights, retained `3MOahpLxj6A` on boundary review hold, and left the film reserve on rights/media hold. The later [source-only group expansion](../../../../plans/reports/rq1-qualification-260929-1925-group-expansion-report.md) passed `oo1ZZlvT2LQ`, bringing current count to N=4/G=4 while preserving the earlier three. This does not lock the cohort or split.

## Cohort and claim conditions

Before cohort lock, audit use in **this project's** candidate training, tuning and model selection, group all videos by transitive shared `course_id` **or any lecturer**, and assign whole groups to train, validation, or sealed final test. An unknown prior use by unrelated parties is disclosed as a limitation, not treated as proof that the sample is universally unused or as an automatic block on further qualification. A proposed final-test video with unclear use in this project's development remains unassigned to final test; it may still be qualified and considered for train/validation. Record the original dataset split as history; it does not automatically define the new split. The entire test group must be absent from training/tuning. Do not divide the current three qualified videos into three one-video splits. Use the [pre-experiment scale/precision plan](rq1-scale-precision-plan-260929.md) before split lock; if it cannot be met, report `blocked_data` and descriptive qualification, not an official effect claim.

Official execution requires per-unit GT, rights for planned internal operations, exact media and timeline, source-bound feature provenance, fixed transcript source, frozen candidate/checkpoint/configuration, D2 manifest, split/history audit, and a separately sealed final-test route. This amendment cannot be used as an eligibility waiver.
