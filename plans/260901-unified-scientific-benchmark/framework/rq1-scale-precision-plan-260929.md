## Original expanded-source recruitment and split target (deferred)

- Target at least **50 independent eligible groups** before official D2/split lock: at least **20 train**, **10 validation**, and **20 sealed final-test** groups. A group may hold multiple videos, but every video in a connected group stays in one split. Train and validation are used to build/tune/select; final-test groups must not enter those steps. These are deferred floors, not an instruction to split the current six groups.
- Before opening the final test, use only train/validation data to estimate the standard deviation `s_val` of **group-level paired F1@3 s deltas** for each predeclared RQ1 contrast. Set the final-test group target to `G_test = max(20, ceil((1.96 * max(s_val) / 0.10)^2))`. This normal approximation is a recruitment calculation, not the final CI. If no stable validation estimate exists, retain the 20-group floor and label precision uncertain; do not claim the ±0.10 target is assured.
- The desired **95% grouped-bootstrap CI half-width is at most 0.10 F1** for the mean of independent-group paired effects. Use 10,000 group resamples for the final CI: compute each group's mean video-level paired delta, then resample those group means. Report video-macro F1/delta separately as the descriptive primary metric and report the actual inferential CI width. If the predeclared group target is unavailable, stop at `blocked_data` for an official generalization claim. If the target is met but the observed CI is wider, report an imprecise official estimate and no claimed effect precision; do not add test groups after seeing their scores.
- Lock all video IDs, within-group multiplicities, exclusions, four contrasts, input versions, candidates, seeds, and group-to-split assignment before any final-test run. Give the same test video cohort to every candidate. Do not use test metrics to change the cohort, model, or rubric.

The 20/10/20 floors protect basic group separation and leave room for development. They do not establish adequate training data for learned C5/C6 candidates; those remain `not_run` until their own train-only checkpoint evidence exists. The planning formula assumes validation group variability is informative; the grouped bootstrap on the sealed test is authoritative.
