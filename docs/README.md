## Retained results

**CVID spotting R5 completed and audited (2026-10-16):** six frozen pipelines were evaluated on the same **1,754 test frames / 40,063 released GT objects**, with all **10,524 checkpoints**, aggregate/runtime/config/source bindings, 48 pooled metric rows and six figures verified. All frames have recorded successful execution; this does not mean correct transcription or valid polygons. One empty docTR checkpoint was recovered from its unchanged sealed aggregate with an audited journal and readback, without rerunning OCR. The [final artifact audit](../experiments/results/cvid-spotting-t4-r5-261016/audit.json) is authoritative; evidence ZIP SHA-256 is `358a51fa96576cbb77820bcbe2177f1b7658556f0e7af1a0355a9276d7b38151`.

PGNet leads pooled E2E Hmean at **45.60%**, followed by docTR **38.13%**, Tesseract **28.40%**, EasyOCR **7.38%**, Paddle DBv3+Recv4 **6.80%** and PP-OCRv6 Medium **6.47%**. PGNet detection P/R/F1 is **81.12/70.44/75.40%**; docTR is **52.28/72.65/60.81%**. Equal-course Hmean narrows the PGNet/docTR gap to **28.01/27.66%**, and docTR leads four of seven course proxies. Native grouping differs substantially: only 11 of 40,063 GT transcriptions are multiword, versus 40.99% of Paddle legacy and 50.51% of PP-OCRv6 output regions. These are packaged-system spotting outcomes, not isolated recognizer rankings. Preserve `no_selection`: EDA-exposed, selectively annotated released GT supports no production winner, unseen-generalization, exhaustive visible-text or CPU/GPU speed claim.
